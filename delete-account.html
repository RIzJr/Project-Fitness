Delete your Project Fitness account

Project Fitness · by Rizalito Jr Mangilit

In the app (straight away)

  1. Open Project Fitness and sign in.
  2. Go to You › Settings (cog) › Cloud account.
  3. Tap Delete my account, then tap it again to confirm.

Your account and everything stored with it are deleted at once: your profile, food logs, workouts, cardio sessions, weigh-ins, check-ins, goals, plans, watch data summaries, Pulse chats and any link with a coach. This can't be undone. Data kept only on your phone (for example progress photos) stays on your phone until you delete the app or use Settings › Data & backup › Delete my data.

Without the app

Email rizalitojrmangilit@yahoo.com from the email address you signed up with, with the subject "Delete my Project Fitness account". Your account and all its data are deleted within 30 days, and you'll get an email when it's done. Nothing is kept afterwards, apart from what the law requires us to keep (we don't currently keep anything for that reason).

Only some of your data

You can delete single entries (meals, workouts, weigh-ins, photos) in the app, clear your Pulse chats in Settings › Pulse, turn off sharing with your coach, or disconnect Apple Health / Health Connect, without deleting your account.

See also the privacy notice and help and support.
