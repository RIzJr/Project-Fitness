Privacy notice

Project Fitness · Last updated 23 October 2026

Who we are

Project Fitness is a fitness and nutrition tracking app run by [your name], a personal trainer based in the UK. For anything about your data, email [contact email].

The short version

  • Your data belongs to you. We don't sell it, share it with advertisers, or use it for anything except running the app.
  • Without an account, everything stays on your phone.
  • With an account, your data is also stored securely online so it syncs between your devices. Only you can read it.
  • A coach only sees weekly summaries, and only if you choose to link with them. You can stop sharing at any time.
  • Progress photos never leave your phone.
  • You can download or delete your data whenever you like.

What we store

  • Account details: your email address and an encrypted password (if you create an account).
  • Profile: name, and optionally phone, date of birth, sex, height, weight, goal, activity level and notes.
  • What you log: food and portions, habits, training sessions, weigh-ins and body measurements, weekly check-ins.
  • Health check answers: your answers to the pre-exercise health questions. This is health information, so we only store it with your explicit agreement.
  • Progress photos: stored only in your phone's browser. They are never uploaded, backed up or shared.

Why we use it, and our legal basis

  • To provide the app and its features (for example, working out your targets and showing your progress): to deliver the service you've asked for.
  • Health information (health check answers, and body measurements where they count as health data): only with your explicit consent, which you give when you tick the box. You can withdraw it at any time by deleting your answers or your account.
  • To share weekly summaries with a coach: only with your consent, when you link with them.

Who can see it

  • You. Your account's data is protected so that only you can read or change it.
  • Your coach, if you link with one. They receive weekly summaries: average weight and measurements, average calories and protein, days on target, habit completion, training sessions and volume, and check-in scores. Your check-in notes and health check result are only included if you tick those options. They never see individual meals, photos, your contact details or your profile notes.
  • Our service providers:
    • Supabase, which hosts the account database and sign-in.
    • GitHub Pages, which hosts the app itself.
    • Open Food Facts, which receives the food names or barcodes you search for (not who you are).
    • Google Fonts, which provides the app's fonts and sees your device's IP address when they load.

How long we keep it

Until you delete it. Deleting your account (Settings → Cloud account → Delete my account) removes your account and everything stored in it straight away, including coach links and shared reports. Copies may remain in our database provider's routine backups for a short period before they are overwritten. If you stop using the app without deleting your account, please email us and we'll delete it.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can:

  • see the data we hold about you
  • get a copy of it (Settings → Data & backup → Download backup)
  • correct it
  • delete it
  • withdraw consent
  • object to how it's used

Most of this you can do yourself in the app. For anything else, email us. If you're unhappy with how we handle your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Under-18s

Project Fitness is designed for adults. Anyone under 18 should only use it with a parent or guardian's permission. Calorie and macro numbers are switched off for under-18s.

Health disclaimer

The health check is a screening questionnaire, not a medical assessment, and calorie suggestions are general estimates. Speak to your GP before starting a new exercise or nutrition plan if you have any health concerns.

Changes

If we change this notice, we'll update the date above and let you know in the app if the change is significant.
