Getting started

  • Without an account: tap Continue without an account. Everything stays on your phone.
  • With an account: sign up with your email to sync between devices and link with your coach.
  • Training with a coach: go to Settings › Cloud account and enter your coach's code. Their planned sessions then appear on Today and Train.
  • Flexible plans: on Train, tap the days you can make it this week under Plan my week. Your sessions stay in order.

Notifications

Turn on reminders in Settings › Reminders. If nothing arrives, check Settings › Notifications › Project Fitness on your iPhone and make sure notifications are allowed.

Your data

You can download a backup from Settings › Data & backup, and delete your account from Settings › Cloud account › Delete my account. Read the privacy notice for the details. See also the terms of use.

Health note

Project Fitness helps you plan and track training and food. It isn't medical advice. If you have a health condition, are pregnant or feel pain while training, check with your GP or a qualified professional first.
